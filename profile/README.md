## SEI Latin America

SEI Latin America is a multidisciplinary research organization focused on water resources, climate resilience, geospatial analysis, environmental monitoring, and applied data science for Latin American contexts.

Our work combines hydrology, machine learning, remote sensing, GIS, and interactive web platforms to support decision-making for water management, ecosystem monitoring, and climate adaptation.

### Organization overview

- Focus areas: hydrology, climate data, geospatial analysis, remote sensing, environmental monitoring, AI for decision support
- Geographic emphasis: Latin America, especially Colombia, Bolivia, Peru, and other regional basins
- Core technologies: Python, R, JavaScript, HTML/CSS, Vue.js, VBA, GIS, Earth Engine, machine learning, Shiny apps
- Typical project types: geoportals, analytical dashboards, hydrologic models, climate exploration platforms, ecosystem monitoring tools

## Final statement

SEI LATAM is building a practical portfolio of applied environmental and water technology solutions, with a strong emphasis on open, evidence-based, and decision-support tools for Latin American contexts. The organization combines scientific research, software engineering, geospatial analysis, and AI-driven workflows to address climate, water, and ecosystem challenges.

We welcome collaboration, open innovation, and partnerships that advance sustainable environmental management and climate resilience.
